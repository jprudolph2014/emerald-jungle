How to play

Earn at least 67% (rounded) to advance. Pass = 1 star, 80% = 2 stars, 100% = 3 stars. Hints are free. Each replay can improve your stars. Bosses are fictional magical guardians; solve equations to power shields, emerald pulses and escape routes.
